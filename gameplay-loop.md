# Core Clicker Loop

1. **Click** — tap to serve a subscriber → cash **only** (no RP from clicks)
2. **Build** — deploy infrastructure (transmission + switching + access)
3. **Research** — RP (passive generation only) unlocks next‑gen gear, multipliers
4. **Expand** — reach penetration → deploy transport → new region
5. **Manage** — give orders to CFO/CTO/COO/CMO. They run departments.
6. **Contracts** — accept external contracts for recurring revenue. Meet their demands or pay penalties.
7. **Finance** — stay private or go public? Buy back shares? Pay dividends?
8. **Network Upgrade (prestige)** — retire obsolete gear → permanent multiplier

## Game Over / Rebirth
- If shareholders remove you or you go bankrupt → **Rebirth** with 30% of previous company value
- Rebirth stacks permanent bonuses. See [rebirth+start.md](rebirth+start.md).

## Currencies

| Currency | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash ($)** | Clicks (cash **only**, no RP), ARPU, interconnect, contracts | Build, auction, R&D, buybacks |
| **Research Points (RP)** | Passive generation (CTO bonus), era events | Tech tree nodes — **never from clicking** |
| **Spectrum Licenses** | Auctions, milestones | Capacity cap |
| **Reputation (⭐)** | Coverage, uptime, contract fulfillment | Better IPO, bond rates, bigger contracts |
| **Ownership %** | Shares you personally hold | Determines control — >50% = safe |
| **Prestige Multiplier** | Era transitions + rebirths | Permanent × income |

## Mobile‑Specific Design
- **Offline earnings** — up to 8h auto revenue
- **Portrait one‑handed** — tap zone + status
- **Notifications** — auctions, contract deadlines, shareholder meetings, crises
- **Monetization** — ad boost (×2 revenue 30m), no‑ads IAP, themes
- **Cloud save** — bind to account

## Summary of Core Systems

| System | Input | Output | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tech Development** | RP + strategic choices | Next‑gen infrastructure, income multipliers | 4 branches × 6 eras, ≈161 nodes with cross‑branch unlock conditions |
| **Market Investment** | Cash + IPO decision + ownership mgmt | Funding, stock price, control vs. cash | IPO risk, buybacks, dividends, hostile takeovers |
| **Infrastructure** | Cash + region targeting | Revenue, coverage, capacity | 40+ asset types, upgrades, congestion, disasters |
| **Company Management** | Executive orders + budgets | Staff-driven bonuses, contract handling | 4 executives, 9 departments, contract system |
| **News System** | Tech unlocks + year triggers | Events with gameplay effects | 2‑layer trigger, 140+ historical events |
| **Rebirth** | Company loss or voluntary | 30% value transfer + stacking bonuses | Permanent per-run progression |
