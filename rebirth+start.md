# Rebirth & New Game+ — Losing & Restarting

## When the CEO Gets Removed

There are two ways you can lose your company:

| Scenario | How It Happens | Triggers |
|---|---|---|
| **Hostile Takeover** | A competitor or activist investor accumulates enough shares and forces a board vote | Your ownership <10%, weak quarterly results, activist investor event |
| **Shareholder Revolt** | Shareholders vote "no confidence" after repeated failures | Your ownership <20%, 3+ bad quarters in a row, dividend cut |
| **Bankruptcy** | Debt exceeds assets for 60 consecutive seconds | Debt > 2× annual revenue, no cash to pay bonds |
| **Acquisition** | A larger company buys a controlling stake and merges you out of existence | Your company is public, a competitor makes an offer, board can accept if your ownership <33% |

### What Happens When You Lose

1. **Your company is gone.** You are removed as CEO. The company is either sold, merged, or restructured.
2. **You keep nothing.** No cash, no infrastructure, no subscribers.
3. **But you have experience.** You can start a **new company** with a head start.

## Rebirth — Start Fresh with 30%

When you lose your company (or choose to do a **Rebirth** from the menu), you can start a new telecom company with **30% of your previous company's total value**.

### What Carries Over (30% of Previous)

| Asset | How It Transfers |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | 30% of your final cash balance becomes starting cash for the new company |
| **Tech Tree Progress** | 30% of researched nodes are randomly retained (not chosen — you keep some knowledge) |
| **Spectrum Licenses** | You lose all. But you get +30% RP for the first 120s (learning from mistakes). |
| **Reputation** | Reset to 0. But you have a "Veteran Founder" badge that unlocks 1 bonus executive slot permanently. |
| **Executive Levels** | Reset to 1. But you can choose 1 executive to start with (any from previous run). |
| **Infrastructure** | All gone. |

### What You Always Keep

| Permanent Unlock | Description |
|---|---|
| **Veteran Founder** | +1 executive slot available (max +3 across all rebirths). Stacks. |
| **Lessons Learned** | All tech tree nodes cost 5% less RP per previous rebirth. Stacks. |
| **Industry Contacts** | Contracts from your previous network appear 2× faster in early eras. |
| **Legacy Name** | Your old company name is recorded in the News Log history. |

### Rebirth vs. Game Over

| Scenario | You Can... |
|---|---|
| **Voluntary Rebirth** | Any time from the menu. You keep 30% and can try a different strategy. |
| **Hostile Takeover / Revolt** | Automatic rebirth with 30%. No choice. |
| **Bankruptcy** | Automatic rebirth with only 15% (penalty for debt mismanagement). |
| **Acquisition (friendly)** | 35% if you negotiated the deal. 20% if forced. |

## Rebirth Progression — Rebirth Multiplier Track

Each rebirth adds to a permanent multiplier that applies across all future runs:

| Rebirth # | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 1st | 5% less RP cost, +1 exec slot, 30% transfer |
| 2nd | 10% less RP cost, +2 exec slots |
| 3rd | 15% less RP cost, +2 exec slots, start with 1 random Era-2 node |
| 4th | 20% less RP cost, +3 exec slots |
| 5th+ | +5% per rebirth (cap 50%), +1 exec slot per 2 rebirths (cap +6) |

---

## Strategy Implications

- **Going public is dangerous** but gives the cash you need for rapid expansion. Stay private if you want to keep 100% control forever.
- **Buybacks are your shield** — use cash to maintain >50% ownership and you are immune to shareholder revolts.
- **Contracts are a double‑edged sword** — they pay well but their demands can break you if you overcommit.
- **Rebirth is not failure** — it's the intended way to stack permanent bonuses and eventually reach Interspace.
