# Finance / Click / Research UI pass

- Click page now contains only Revenue, Quality, Coverage, and Connections.
- Infrastructure-unlocking research nodes have an amber dashed outline and marker; tooltips name the unlocked asset.
- Bottom IPO navigation was renamed Finance.
- Finance has IPO and Bank subpages.
- Bank provides a revolving credit line with a minimum $500 limit or six months of current network revenue, whichever is larger.
- Bank APR starts at 3.00%, rerolls every three game months, and remains between 2.70% and 3.30%.
- Interest accrues monthly to outstanding debt. Players can borrow 25%, 50%, or all available credit and repay 25% or as much as possible.
