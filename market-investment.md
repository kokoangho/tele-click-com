# Market Investment — Realistic Telecom Finance

## Financial Layers
- **Click Revenue** — tap to "connect a call / serve a subscriber" = instant cash
- **Monthly ARPU** — idle revenue per subscriber, varies by access tech: POTS $20 → Broadband $50 → 5G $70 → Interspace $500
- **Interconnect fees** — revenue from carrying other carriers' traffic, +5–15%
- **Spectrum** — real cost: US AWS-3 auction 2015 = $81B. Winning spectrum costs cash

## Investment Timeline

| Stage | Era | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Founder Equity | 1 | Bootstrapped, family & friends |
| Revenue Bonds | 2 | First debt, backed by subscriber revenue |

## IPO — The Great Gamble

Going public is **optional**. You can stay private forever. But the IPO gives a massive one‑time cash injection that can accelerate your company past competitors.

### IPO Mechanics

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cash raised at IPO** | Company valuation × % shares sold. A typical IPO sells 20–40% of the company. |
| **Your ownership after IPO** | 100% – % sold. If you sell 30%, you keep 70%. |
| **Valuation formula** | (Annual revenue × 3) + (subscriber count × $500) + (spectrum value) × reputation bonus |
| **Not going public** | You keep 100% control. No shareholder pressure. But slower cash growth. |

### The Danger — Shareholder Control

Once public, you answer to shareholders. They can file demands, and if enough of them agree, they can **remove you as CEO** or **force a sale** of the company.

**Shareholder Revolt Thresholds:**

| Your Ownership % | What Shareholders Can Do |
|---|---|
| >50% | **Full control.** You cannot be removed. Shareholder votes are advisory only. |
| 33–50% | **Majority holder.** Can block hostile actions but must negotiate on dividends. |
| 20–33% | **Significant minority.** Shareholders can call emergency votes. Board seats contested. |
| 10–20% | **Weak founder.** Shareholders can force strategic changes, replace CFO/COO. |
| <10% | **At risk.** If shareholders vote "no confidence", you are removed as CEO. Company is sold. |

**Hostile Takeover Process:**
1. An activist investor or competitor accumulates shares (you can fight with buybacks)
2. If they reach 15%+ ownership, they can call a special board meeting
3. Board votes on CEO removal — your ownership % determines sway
4. If removed → you lose the company → see `rebirth+start.md`

### Staying Private: Pros & Cons

| | Private | Public |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | Limited to bonds & VC | IPO cash + stock offerings |
| **Control** | 100% yours | Shareholders have a say |
| **Risk** | Slower growth | Can lose the company |
| **M&A** | Cash-only acquisitions | Can use stock as currency |
| **Dividends** | Not expected | Expected by shareholders |
| **Reporting** | None | Quarterly earnings, transparency |

### Stock Mechanics (post‑IPO)
Price driven by quarterly reports:

| Metric | Impact |
|---|---|
| Subscriber net adds | +/– ARPU change |
| Churn rate | Negative pressure |
| CapEx / Revenue ratio | Efficiency signal |
| Spectrum win | +market confidence |
| Dividend yield | Attracts income investors |
| Debt / EBITDA | Leverage risk |
| **Your ownership %** | Higher = more stable stock (confidence in leadership) |

### Managing Shareholders
- **Buybacks** — spend cash to buy shares from market → increase your ownership %
- **Dividends** — pay out profits → keep shareholders happy, reduce revolt chance
- **Shareholder meetings** — every quarter, you present results. Bad results = revolt risk increases
- **Poison pill** — if a competitor tries to acquire >10%, automatically issue new shares to dilute (tech tree unlock: corporate law)

## Random Market Events

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Spectrum Auction** — govt releases new band | Bid cash or lose capacity |
| **Competitor Merger** — rival consolidation | Industry consolidation premium |
| **Net Neutrality Ruling** — regulation flip | Changes zero‑rating ability |
| **Infrastructure Stimulus** — govt broadband | Free fiber in rural regions |
| **Recession** — enterprise spending cut | Revenue –30% for 60s |
| **Patent Dispute** — royalty demand | Cash hit or cross‑license |
| **Natural Disaster** — hurricane / quake | Repair cost, outage penalties |
| **Activist Investor** — fund buys 5% of your stock | Pressure to split company or sell |
| **Shareholder Revolt** — poor quarterly results | Must increase dividend or face CEO vote |

## Strategic Tension
Reinvest → R&D & infra growth vs. Buyback → protect ownership vs. Dividend → appease shareholders vs. Hoard → spectrum war chest vs. Stay private → keep control, grow slower
